# Larkspur Billing FAQ

Larkspur Mutual Life is a fictional company. This document is synthetic sample data written for a retrieval benchmark.

## Payment Modes
Premiums can be paid annually, semiannually, quarterly, or monthly. Each payment is the annual premium multiplied by a modal factor: 1.0 for annual, 0.52 for semiannual, 0.265 for quarterly, and 0.0875 for monthly. Monthly payment is available only by automatic bank draft.

## Policy Fee
Every policy carries a policy fee of $60 per year. The fee is part of the annual premium, so it is included before the modal factor is applied. The policy fee is not charged on riders.

## Late Payment Notices
Larkspur mails a late payment notice 10 days after a missed due date. The notice states the last day of the grace period for that policy.

## Returned Payments
A payment returned by the bank carries a fee of $25. After two returned payments in any 12-month period, Larkspur removes the policy from automatic bank draft and bills it quarterly.

## Changing the Draft Date
The owner may choose any draft date from the 1st through the 28th of the month. The draft date can be changed once in any 12-month period.

## Refunds on Cancellation
If a term policy is cancelled after the free look period, Larkspur refunds the unearned part of the premium on a pro rata basis. No refund of premium is made when a whole life policy is surrendered; the owner receives the cash surrender value instead.
