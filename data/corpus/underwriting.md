# Larkspur Underwriting Guide

Larkspur Mutual Life is a fictional company. This document is synthetic sample data written for a retrieval benchmark.

## Risk Classes
Larkspur assigns every approved applicant to one of four risk classes: Preferred Plus, Preferred, Standard, or Tobacco. Preferred Plus requires excellent health and no adverse family history. Preferred allows minor, well-controlled conditions. Standard is average health for the applicant's age. Tobacco applies to any applicant who meets the tobacco definition, whatever their other health factors.

## Tobacco Definition
An applicant is rated as a tobacco user if they have used any nicotine product within the past 12 months. This includes cigarettes, vaping products, chewing tobacco, and nicotine replacement such as patches or gum. An applicant who smokes no more than 12 cigars a year and tests negative for nicotine may be considered for non-tobacco rates.

## Simplified (No-Exam) Underwriting
Applicants aged 18 through 50 may apply for face amounts up to $500,000 without a medical exam. The decision is based on the application, prescription history, and motor vehicle records, and is usually made within 2 business days.

## Medical Exam Requirements
A paramedical exam with blood and urine samples is required for any face amount above $500,000, and for every applicant aged 51 or older. Applicants aged 51 or older applying for more than $1,000,000 also need a resting electrocardiogram. Larkspur pays for the exam. Exam results stay valid for 90 days.

## Financial Underwriting
Total life insurance in force with all companies may not exceed a multiple of the applicant's annual income. The multiple is 30 times income at ages 18 through 40, 20 times at ages 41 through 50, 15 times at ages 51 through 60, and 10 times at age 61 and older.

## Rate Reconsideration
An insured rated as Tobacco may ask for reconsideration after 12 consecutive months without nicotine use. Larkspur requires a new nicotine test. If the request is approved, the lower premium starts on the next premium due date.
