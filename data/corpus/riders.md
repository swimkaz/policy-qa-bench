# Larkspur Optional Riders

Larkspur Mutual Life is a fictional company. This document is synthetic sample data written for a retrieval benchmark.

## Waiver of Premium Rider
If the insured becomes totally disabled and stays disabled for 6 consecutive months, Larkspur waives all premiums that fall due while the disability continues. Premiums paid during those first 6 months are refunded. The disability must begin before the insured reaches age 60. The rider can be added at issue ages 18 through 55.

## Accelerated Death Benefit Rider
If a physician certifies that the insured has a terminal illness with a life expectancy of 12 months or less, the owner may receive part of the death benefit early. The maximum advance is 75% of the face amount, up to $500,000. There is no additional premium for this rider. Larkspur charges an administrative fee of $150 when the benefit is used. The amount advanced reduces the death benefit paid later.

## Child Term Rider
The Child Term Rider provides $10,000 of term coverage on each eligible child. A child is eligible from 15 days old until their 18th birthday, and coverage on a covered child continues to age 25. One premium of $72 per year covers all eligible children. At age 25 a covered child may convert to as much as $50,000 of permanent coverage without evidence of insurability.

## Accidental Death Benefit Rider
If the insured dies as the direct result of an accident, and the death occurs within 90 days of the accident, this rider pays an extra amount equal to the face amount, up to $250,000. The rider expires on the policy anniversary nearest the insured's 70th birthday. It does not cover deaths resulting from war, the commission of a felony, or intoxication.

## Guaranteed Insurability Rider
This rider is available on whole life policies only. It lets the owner buy up to $50,000 of additional coverage on each option date without evidence of insurability. Option dates are the policy anniversaries nearest ages 25, 28, 31, 34, 37 and 40.
