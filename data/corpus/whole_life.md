# Larkspur Whole Life: Policy Summary

Larkspur Mutual Life is a fictional company. This document is synthetic sample data written for a retrieval benchmark.

## Overview
Larkspur Whole Life provides coverage for the insured's entire life. Premiums are level and payable to age 100. The policy builds guaranteed cash value and is eligible for dividends. Face amounts range from $25,000 to $5,000,000, and the policy is issued at ages 0 through 80.

## Free Look Period
The owner may return a whole life policy for any reason within 10 days of delivery for a refund of every premium paid.

## Grace Period
A whole life premium not paid by its due date may be paid during a grace period of 61 days. Coverage continues during the grace period. If the premium remains unpaid at the end of the grace period, the policy lapses unless its cash value supports a nonforfeiture option.

## Cash Value
Guaranteed cash value starts to accumulate at the end of the second policy year. It grows at a guaranteed minimum interest rate of 2.5% per year. The cash surrender value is the cash value minus any surrender charge and any outstanding policy loan.

## Policy Loans
Once a whole life policy has cash value, the owner may borrow up to 90% of the cash surrender value. Loan interest is 5.5% per year, charged at the end of each policy year. Unpaid interest is added to the loan balance. If the insured dies with a loan outstanding, the death benefit is reduced by the loan balance and accrued interest. If the loan balance grows larger than the cash value, the policy terminates 31 days after Larkspur mails a notice.

## Surrender Charges
A surrender charge applies if a whole life policy is surrendered in its first five years. The charge is a percentage of the cash value: 10% in policy year 1, 8% in year 2, 6% in year 3, 4% in year 4, and 2% in year 5. No surrender charge applies from policy year 6 onward.

## Dividends
Whole life policies are eligible for annual dividends. Dividends are not guaranteed. The owner may take a dividend in cash, use it to reduce the premium, use it to buy paid-up additions, or leave it to accumulate at interest. If the owner makes no choice, dividends buy paid-up additions.

## Reinstatement
A lapsed whole life policy can be reinstated within 5 years of the lapse date. The owner must provide evidence of insurability and pay all overdue premiums with interest at 6% per year, and must repay or restore any policy loan.
