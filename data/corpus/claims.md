# Larkspur Claims Guide

Larkspur Mutual Life is a fictional company. This document is synthetic sample data written for a retrieval benchmark.

## How to File a Claim
A beneficiary can start a claim by phone or through the online claims portal. Larkspur needs a completed claim form, a certified copy of the death certificate, and the policy number. Each beneficiary must also provide government-issued photo identification.

## Processing Time
Larkspur pays a claim within 30 days of receiving complete proof of loss. Most claims with complete documents are paid within 10 business days.

## Interest on Delayed Payment
If a claim is not paid within 30 days of receiving complete proof of loss, Larkspur adds interest at 8% per year, calculated from the date of death.

## Claims During the Contestable Period
If the insured dies during the first 2 years after the policy's issue date, the claim is reviewed before payment. Larkspur compares the application with the insured's medical records. This contestable review can add up to 60 additional days to the normal processing time.

## Payout Options
A beneficiary may take the death benefit as a single lump sum, as installments over a fixed period of 5 to 20 years, as a life income, or may leave it in an interest-bearing retained asset account.

## Beneficiaries
The owner may name primary and contingent beneficiaries. If no named beneficiary is living when the insured dies, the death benefit is paid to the insured's estate. A benefit payable to a minor is paid to a court-appointed guardian or a custodian named under a transfers-to-minors law. A beneficiary change takes effect on the date the owner signed the change form, once Larkspur has recorded it.
