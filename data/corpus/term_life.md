# Larkspur Term Life: Policy Summary

Larkspur Mutual Life is a fictional company. This document is synthetic sample data written for a retrieval benchmark.

## Overview
Larkspur Term Life pays a death benefit if the insured dies during the level term. Premiums stay level for the whole term. The policy builds no cash value and pays no dividends. Face amounts range from $100,000 to $2,000,000.

## Term Lengths and Issue Ages
Three level terms are offered. The 10-year term is issued at ages 18 through 70. The 20-year term is issued at ages 18 through 60. The 30-year term is issued at ages 18 through 50. Age means the applicant's age on their last birthday at the application date.

## Free Look Period
The owner may return a term policy for any reason within 20 days of delivery. Larkspur then cancels the policy from its issue date and refunds every premium paid.

## Grace Period
A term premium not paid by its due date may still be paid during a grace period of 31 days. Coverage stays in force during the grace period. If the insured dies during the grace period, the overdue premium is deducted from the death benefit. If the premium is still unpaid when the grace period ends, the policy lapses.

## Reinstatement
A lapsed term policy can be reinstated within 3 years of the lapse date. The owner must provide evidence of insurability that satisfies Larkspur and pay all overdue premiums with interest at 6% per year.

## Conversion Privilege
A term policy can be converted to a Larkspur Whole Life policy without a medical exam. The conversion must happen before the earlier of the end of the 10th policy year or the policy anniversary nearest the insured's 65th birthday. The premium for the new policy is based on the insured's age at the time of conversion.

## Contestability
Larkspur may contest a term policy for a material misstatement in the application during the first 2 years after the issue date. After 2 years the policy is incontestable, except for nonpayment of premium. If the insured's age was misstated, the death benefit is adjusted to the amount the premium paid would have bought at the correct age.

## Suicide Exclusion
If the insured dies by suicide within 2 years of the issue date, Larkspur does not pay the death benefit. Larkspur instead refunds all premiums paid to the beneficiary.

## Renewal After the Level Term
When the level term ends, the policy may be renewed one year at a time up to age 95. Renewal premiums increase every year and are much higher than the level premium.
